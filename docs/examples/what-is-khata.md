# What is Khata

Khata is a digital ledger for recording credit given to regular customers.
It replaces the paper udhaar book, and both you and the customer can see the
running balance at any time.

## Setting a credit limit

Each customer can have a credit limit set from the customer detail screen.
When a customer reaches their limit, new khata entries are blocked until they
settle part of the balance.

## Khata reminders

Payment reminders can be sent over WhatsApp or SMS. Reminders are sent manually
from the customer screen; there is no automatic reminder schedule.
