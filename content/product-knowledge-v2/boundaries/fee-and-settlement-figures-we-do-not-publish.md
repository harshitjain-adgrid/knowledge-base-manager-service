# Fee and Settlement Figures Chotu Cannot Quote

Some numbers about fees and settlement timing are not published to the
assistant. This document exists so that a question about them gets a straight
"we do not have that figure" rather than a guess that sounds authoritative.

## The figures that are not available here

- **The fee or MDR percentage** taken on a payment, and how it differs by
  payment instrument.
- **The settlement cycle** — whether money moves same day, next working day, or
  on some other rhythm.
- **The settlement cut-off time**, after which a payment goes in the next batch.
- **The minimum settlement amount**, if there is one.
- **How long a pending payment waits** before it is marked failed.

None of these are secret. They are simply not among the facts loaded here, and a
figure invented to sound helpful would be worse than no figure at all.

## Where the real number is

Your own screens carry the figures that apply to your account:

- Your **settlements screen** shows your actual schedule and when the next
  transfer goes out.
- An individual **payment receipt** shows the amount that came in and the
  amount after fees, which is the fee on that payment in rupees.

Those are specific to you and they are accurate. A general percentage quoted
from memory would not be.

## What Chotu will do instead

Asked for one of these figures, Chotu says it does not have it and points at the
screen that does, or at support. It does not estimate, does not offer a typical
industry rate, and does not reason from an example.

This is deliberate. A merchant planning their pricing around an invented MDR
would be worse off than a merchant told to go and look.

## Hinglish mein

Kuch numbers Chotu ke paas hain hi nahi, jaise payment par kitna fee ya MDR
katta hai, settlement kitne din mein aata hai, cut-off time kya hai, aur koi
minimum amount hai ya nahi. Yeh chhupaye nahi gaye hain, bas yahan load nahi
kiye gaye. Aise sawal par Chotu saaf mana kar dega, andaza nahi lagayega.
Sahi number aapki apni screen par milta hai: settlement screen par aapka
schedule aur agli transfer kab jayegi, aur kisi bhi payment ki receipt par
kitna aaya aur fees ke baad kitna bacha. Wahi aapke account ke liye sach hai.
