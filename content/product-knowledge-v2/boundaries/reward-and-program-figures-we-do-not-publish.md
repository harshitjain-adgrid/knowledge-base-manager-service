# Reward and Program Figures Chotu Cannot Quote

Several exact amounts in the loyalty and program areas are not published to the
assistant. The rules around them are documented and answerable; the numbers
themselves are not.

## The figures that are not available here

- **How many coins** a customer earns for a referral or a payment.
- **How many credits** a merchant earns for a referral, and whether there is a
  cap on how many referrals count.
- **How long coins or credits last** before they expire, and how many days
  ahead the expiry reminder is sent.
- **What a coin is worth** in rupees, and whether there is a limit on how many
  a customer may redeem in one payment.
- **What counts as the qualifying first action** that releases a referral
  reward.
- **What the Champion Program tablet costs**, and whether a deposit is involved
  or what it is.
- **Who is eligible** for the Champion Program and how enrolment is decided.
- **How many digits the PIN is**, and the rules on what it may contain.
- **The names and prices of the placement tiers**, which are still being
  decided.

## The rule is documented even where the number is not

This is worth separating, because the two often arrive in the same question.
How a referral reward works — that it is paid after the referred person acts
rather than at signup, that it lands as reward credit, that reward credit
expires — is all documented and Chotu can explain it.

The amount is the part that is missing. So *"why has my referral reward not
arrived"* has a real answer, and *"how much is it"* does not.

## What Chotu will do instead

Asked for one of these amounts, Chotu says it does not have the figure. It does
not infer one from an example, and it does not treat a number that appears in a
sample message as a real rate.

Your own screens carry what actually applies to you: your wallet shows the
credit you have received, and your referrals screen shows what each referral
earned you.

## Hinglish mein

Kuch exact numbers Chotu ke paas nahi hain — jaise ek referral par kitne coins
ya credit milte hain, koi limit hai ya nahi, coins aur credits kitne din chalte
hain, ek coin ki keemat kitni hai, Champion Program ka tablet kitne
ka hai ya uska deposit kya hai, kaun is program ke liye eligible hai, aur PIN
kitne digit ka hota hai. Dhyan dene wali baat yeh hai ki niyam documented hain,
sirf number nahi. Referral ka reward kab milta hai, kis roop mein milta hai aur
kab expire hota hai — yeh sab Chotu bata sakta hai. Kitna milta hai, yeh nahi.
Aapke wallet aur referrals screen par aapke apne account ka asli hisaab dikhta
hai.
