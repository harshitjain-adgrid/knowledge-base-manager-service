# Referring Other Shops

If you bring another shopkeeper onto LessPay, you earn a reward for it. You have
a code of your own, they use it when they sign up, and credit lands in your
wallet once their shop is running. There is nothing to apply for and no limit on
how many people you can bring.

## The steps to refer a shop

1. Open **Referrals** in LessPay Business to find your referral code. It exists
   as soon as your account does — there is nothing to set up.
2. Share the code with the shopkeeper directly.
3. They enter it while signing up for LessPay.
4. They finish setting up their shop and get it running.
5. Your reward lands in the **rewards** bucket of your wallet.

## What you earn

Referral rewards arrive as **reward credit** in your wallet. That is the bucket
you can spend on LessPay's own promotion channels.

Reward credit is not the same as credit you buy — it is limited to LessPay's own
channels and it carries an expiry date. It is worth spending rather than saving.

## Watching your referrals

Your referrals screen shows your code, how many people have joined through it,
and what you have earned. It is the place to check before assuming a reward has
gone missing.

## Not this

This is a shopkeeper bringing **another shopkeeper** onto LessPay. A merchant
referring their own **customers** into the customer app is a different scheme
with a different reward, paid in coins to the customer.

## What if a referral has not been credited

- **They may not have used your code.** Signing up without it means there is
  nothing linking the account to you.
- **They may not have finished setting up.** A reward follows a shop that is
  actually up and running, not one that started signing up and stopped.
- **The reward may already be there.** Check the rewards bucket of your wallet
  rather than the total, since it is credited there.

## Hinglish mein

Agar aap kisi doosre dukaandar ko LessPay par laate hain to aapko iska inaam
milta hai. Referrals mein jaakar apna referral code dekhein — yeh account
banate hi ban jata hai, kuch alag se karna nahi padta. Code un tak pahunchayein,
wo sign up karte waqt daalein, aur jab unki dukaan chalu ho jaye to aapke wallet
ke rewards wale hisse mein credit aa jata hai. Yeh reward credit khareede hue
credit se alag hai — iski expiry hoti hai aur yeh sirf LessPay ke apne channel
par lagta hai, isliye ise bachane se behtar hai kharch karna. Kitne log aaye
aur kitna mila, yeh referrals screen par dikhta hai.
