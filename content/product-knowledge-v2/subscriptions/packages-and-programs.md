# Packages and Programs

Beyond the free parts of LessPay, you can subscribe to paid bundles. They come in
two shapes and the difference matters when you are deciding what to buy.

## Packages

A **package** is a bundle you subscribe to for a period. It gives you things
like promotion credits, a better position in customer listings, or access to
features you would otherwise pay for one at a time.

Packages renew on their own schedule and their benefits arrive on that schedule
rather than the day you ask for them.

## Programs

A **program** is a service LessPay runs for you rather than a bundle of credits.
The typical example is hardware or a managed service — something LessPay
provides and maintains on your behalf.

Programs can involve a **deposit**, which packages do not.

## What you can see before subscribing

Every package and program shows you what it costs, what you get, how long it
runs, and what the charges are made of, before you commit to anything. Nothing
is charged until you confirm.

## Your active subscriptions

Your subscriptions screen shows what you are currently on, when it renews, and
what benefits it carries. It is worth checking before buying promotion credits
separately — a package you already have may include them.

## What if you want to stop

A subscription runs to the end of the period you paid for. Stopping it prevents
the next renewal rather than cutting off what you have already bought.

If a program involved a deposit, that deposit comes back when the equipment is
returned in working order; it is not part of what you spent on the service.

## Hinglish mein

Free cheezon ke alawa LessPay par paid bundle bhi milte hain aur wo do tarah ke
hote hain. Package matlab ek bundle jo aap kuch samay ke liye lete hain — isme
promotion ke credit, listing mein behtar jagah, ya kuch features milte hain, aur
inke faayde package ke apne schedule par aate hain. Program matlab koi service
jo LessPay aapke liye chalata hai, jaise hardware ya koi managed service.
Program mein deposit lag sakta hai, package mein nahi. Kuch bhi lene se pehle
poora kharcha, faayda aur samay dikh jata hai. Band karne par jo aapne pehle
se paisa diya hai wo chalta rehta hai, sirf agli renewal ruk jati hai.
