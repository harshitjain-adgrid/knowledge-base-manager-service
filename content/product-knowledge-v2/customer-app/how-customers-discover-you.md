# How Customers Discover You

The LessPay customer app is built for shoppers to find merchants near them who
are running offers. Understanding how they arrive at your shop explains which
parts of your setup actually do work.

## The three ways in

- **The home feed.** The customer app's home screen shows merchants and offers,
  arranged by LessPay. This is where a paid placement puts you.
- **Search.** A customer types what they want and gets results, which they can
  narrow with filters and sorting. Your name and your category decide whether
  you appear.
- **Scanning your QR.** Somebody already standing in your shop. No discovery
  needed — this is the counter, not the app.

## What a customer sees on your page

Opening your shop in the app shows them three things: your shop details, the
offers you are running, and your reviews.

All three are worth attention. Details tell them what you are, offers give them
a reason, and reviews decide whether they believe it.

## Offers are what turn a listing into a visit

The app is organised around merchants *who are running offers*. A shop with
nothing on is a shop with nothing to show in a feed built around deals.

Being visible gets you looked at. An offer is what gets you visited.

## Reviews are part of your listing

Customers can rate and review you after paying, and those reviews sit on your
shop page alongside your offers. You do not control them, and they are visible
to everyone deciding whether to come.

## What if customers are not finding you

- **Your category may be wrong or unset.** It is one of the ways browsing
  customers narrow down, so a shop in "Service & others" is hard to stumble on.
- **You may have no live offer.** Feeds and lists built around deals skip shops
  without one.
- **You may be relying on standard placement** in an area with a lot of shops.
  That is what paid placement is for.
- **You may simply be far** from where the customer is looking. Discovery is
  anchored to where they are.

## Hinglish mein

Customer aap tak teen tarah se pahunchta hai: app ke home feed se, search se, ya
seedha dukaan par aakar QR scan karke. Aapke page par teen cheezein dikhti hain
— dukaan ki detail, chal rahe offer, aur reviews. App un dukaano ke aas paas
bana hai jo offer chala rahe hain, isliye bina offer ke dikhna mushkil hai.
Category sahi rakhna zaroori hai, warna browse karne wale customer ko aap nahi
milenge.
