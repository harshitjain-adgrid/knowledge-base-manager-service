# How a Customer Pays You

At the counter, paying is four actions and the offer is applied for them. Nothing
is typed in by you, and no coupon code changes hands. This matters because it is
the moment your offers actually do their work.

## What happens at the counter

1. The customer **scans your QR code**. The app resolves it to your shop.
2. They **type the bill amount**.
3. The app works out which of your offers that amount has unlocked, and applies
   the **best one automatically**.
4. They **pay**, and get a receipt. They can leave a review afterwards.

## The best offer is chosen for them

The customer is never asked to pick. As they type the amount, the app checks
which of your offers apply at that amount and applies the single most valuable
one.

Two consequences worth knowing: a customer cannot accidentally choose a worse
offer, and they also cannot combine two — only one applies.

## The discount happens at pay time

There is no coupon to remember, no code to enter, and nothing for the customer
to have collected in advance. The offer is worked out at the moment of payment
from the offers you have live.

This is why an offer that is scheduled or inactive does nothing at the counter —
the app is reading your live offers as the customer types.

## The app nudges them upwards, but honestly

If spending a little more would unlock a better offer, the app says so — *"add
₹120 more and save 20%"*.

It only does this when the customer would genuinely be better off. It will never
suggest spending more for a smaller saving, which is worth knowing before you
design an offer ladder that assumes otherwise.

## Scanning shows them your offers, not just your name

When the QR is scanned, what comes back is your shop **and the offers this
particular customer is eligible for**. Two customers scanning the same QR can
see different offers, because audiences and limits are applied per customer.

## What if the discount did not come off

- **The amount was below the threshold** the offer needs.
- **A different offer applied** because it saved them more.
- **The offer is not live** — scheduled, inactive or expired.
- **They did not qualify** for the audience it was aimed at.

## Hinglish mein

Counter par customer aapka QR scan karta hai, bill ki rakam likhta hai, aur app
khud hi sabse acha offer laga deta hai — customer ko chunna nahi padta aur na
hi koi coupon code daalna padta hai. Ek waqt mein ek hi offer lagta hai. Agar
thoda zyada kharch karne par behtar offer milta hai to app khud batata hai. Jo
offer live nahi hai wo counter par nahi lagega, isliye scheduled ya inactive
offer ka koi asar nahi hota.
