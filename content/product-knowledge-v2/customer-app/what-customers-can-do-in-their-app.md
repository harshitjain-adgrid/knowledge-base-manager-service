# What Customers Can Do in Their App

Knowing what your customers can actually do explains a lot of what you see from
your side — why someone comes back, why a review appears, why a customer asks
about coins.

## Their side of the app

- **Find shops** near them that are running offers, by browsing or searching.
- **Pay** by scanning a merchant's QR, with the best offer applied for them.
- **Earn coins**, which build up in a wallet they can see and spend.
- **Save favourites**, so shops they like are easy to return to.
- **Rate and review** a merchant after paying.
- **Refer friends**, which is how coins and credits get earned on both sides.

## Coins are their reward for using LessPay

Customers earn coins through LessPay — most visibly through referrals — and they
build up in a wallet with a history they can look at.

Coins are between the customer and LessPay. They are not funded by you, they do
not come out of anything of yours, and a customer spending coins is not you
giving a discount.

## Favourites are a real signal

A customer can save your shop, which puts you one tap away next time. It is
worth knowing that this exists, because it means a good first visit has somewhere
to go — a customer who favourites you has made returning easy for themselves.

## Reviews arrive after payment

The review prompt comes after a payment, which is why reviews on LessPay tend to
be from people who actually bought something. They appear on your shop page for
every future customer to read.

## Not this — coins are not your credits

**Coins** belong to customers and are spent on their purchases. **Credits**
belong to you and sit on your merchant account. A customer asking about their
coin balance is asking about something you cannot see or adjust. For the
difference, see "Coins and Credits — Who Holds What".

## Hinglish mein

Customer apne app mein aas paas ki dukaanein dhoondh sakta hai, QR scan karke
payment kar sakta hai, coins kama sakta hai, dukaan ko favourite mein save kar
sakta hai, payment ke baad review de sakta hai, aur doston ko refer kar sakta
hai. Coins customer ke apne hote hain — wo aapke paise se nahi katte. Review
payment ke baad hi maanga jata hai, isliye review dene wale wahi log hote hain
jinhone sach mein kuch khareeda ho.
