# Coins and Credits — Who Holds What

LessPay runs two loyalty currencies and they belong to different people.
Confusing them is the most common mix-up in this area, so it is worth getting
straight once: **customers hold coins, merchants hold credits.**

## The difference in one table

| | Coins | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Held by | Customers | Merchants — you |
| Earned from | Referrals and rewards in the customer app | Referrals and rewards on your account |
| Spent on | Purchases the customer makes | Things on your merchant account |
| Can expire | Yes | Yes |

## Coins are not your money

Coins sit in a customer's account and are theirs to spend. They do not come out
of your wallet, you do not fund them, and you cannot see or manage another
person's coins.

When a customer pays you partly with coins, that is between the customer and
LessPay — it is not a discount you are giving.

## Credits are yours

Credits are the balance on your own account. They are what you earn from LessPay
rather than from customers, most commonly by referring people.

## Both of them expire

Neither currency lasts forever. Coins and credits both carry a validity period,
and when it passes, the unspent balance is gone.

You are warned before it happens — a reminder goes out ahead of the expiry date,
and a second message when the balance actually lapses. Those messages are the
practical way to stay on top of it rather than checking a screen.

## Not this — credits are not a bank balance

Credits are a **reward balance on your LessPay account** and they expire. The
money customers pay you is different: it is real money, it settles to your bank
account, and it does not expire. A large credit balance is not money you can
withdraw.

## Hinglish mein

Do alag alag cheezein hain aur log inhe aksar mila dete hain. Coins customer ke
paas hote hain — wo apni kharidari mein lagate hain, aur wo aapke paise nahi
hain. Credits merchant ke paas hote hain, yaani aapke account par. Dono expire
hote hain, aur expire hone se pehle aapko yaad dilane wala message aata hai.
Credits bank ka paisa nahi hai — customer ka diya hua asli paisa alag hota hai
aur wo bank mein settle hota hai.
