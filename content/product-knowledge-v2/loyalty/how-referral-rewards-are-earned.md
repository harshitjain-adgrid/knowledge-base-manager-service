# How Referral Rewards Are Earned

You can refer customers to LessPay, and when a referral works out, both sides
are rewarded — the customer gets coins, and you get credits. The timing is the
part worth understanding, because it is not at signup.

## The steps to earn a referral reward

1. Share your referral with a customer.
2. They sign up to LessPay using it.
3. You are told they have joined, and that your reward is pending.
4. They complete their **first qualifying action**.
5. Your credits are added, and you are told they have arrived.

## The reward unlocks on the first action, not the signup

This is the rule that surprises people. A customer signing up is not enough. The
reward is released once they actually *do* something — their first qualifying
action on the platform.

There is a good reason for it: rewarding signups alone is trivially easy to
abuse, and a scheme that pays out for accounts that never do anything ends up
paying for nothing and gets withdrawn for everybody.

## You are told at both points

You get two separate messages, and they mean different things:

- **When they sign up** — a notice that the person joined and that your reward
  is waiting on their first action.
- **When it is released** — a notice that the credits are now on your account.

Seeing the first and not the second means the customer has joined but not yet
done the thing that releases it.

## Both sides are rewarded, in different currencies

The customer receives **coins** in their account. You receive **credits** on
yours. They are not the same currency and they are not interchangeable.

## What if your referral reward has not arrived

- **They have joined but not acted.** This is the usual case, and the first
  message you received said so.
- **They did not use your referral.** A signup with no referral attached is not
  linked to you and cannot be credited afterwards.
- **Check your credits, not your bank.** Referral rewards arrive as credits on
  your LessPay account, not as money in your bank account.

## Hinglish mein

Aap customer ko LessPay par refer kar sakte hain. Jab wo aapke referral se
signup karta hai to aapko ek message aata hai ki wo jud gaya hai, lekin reward
abhi pending hai. Reward tab milta hai jab wo customer apna pehla asli kaam
karta hai — sirf account banane se nahi milta, taki koi galat fayda na uthaye.
Customer ko coins milte hain aur aapko credits milte hain. Credits aapke LessPay
account par aate hain, bank mein nahi.
