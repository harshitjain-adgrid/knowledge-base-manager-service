# What the Champion Program Is

The Champion Program is an arrangement under which LessPay installs a tablet in
your shop. It is not open to everyone — merchants are selected into it. Shop
owners usually ask about it as *tablet wala program* or just *champion*.

## What it actually is

A LessPay tablet, installed at your counter, which you use to take payments and
run offers from a screen that belongs to LessPay rather than from your own
phone.

Getting into the program is a lifecycle rather than a purchase: you are
enrolled, the tablet is dispatched to you, delivered, installed and activated,
and then you are live on it.

## It is a program, not a feature you switch on

You do not turn the Champion Program on from a settings screen. Merchants are
selected for it, and enrolment is what starts everything else.

If you have not been enrolled, there is nothing in the app to enable — the right
question is whether your shop is eligible, and that is a conversation with
LessPay rather than a toggle.

## You are told at every stage

The gap between being enrolled and having a working tablet involves shipping and
a visit, so LessPay fills that silence deliberately: each stage of the journey
sends you a message, from dispatch through to going live.

That means you should never have to ask where your tablet is — the last message
you received is the answer.

## Not this — a tablet is not a paid placement

The Champion Program puts **hardware in your shop**. Paid placement buys your
deal a **more visible position in the customer app**. They are separate things
you can have independently: a tablet does not make your offers more visible, and
buying visibility does not get you a tablet. For placement, see "Where Your Deal
Appears".

## Hinglish mein

Champion Program matlab LessPay aapki dukaan mein ek tablet lagata hai, jispar
aap payment le sakte hain aur offer chala sakte hain. Ye sabke liye nahi hai —
LessPay khud merchant chunta hai. Enrol hone ke baad tablet bheja jata hai,
deliver hota hai, install hota hai, aur phir aap live ho jate hain. Har stage
par aapko message aata hai, to tablet kahan pahuncha hai ye poochne ki zaroorat
nahi padti.
