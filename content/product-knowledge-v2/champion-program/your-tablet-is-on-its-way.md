# Tracking Your Champion Program Tablet

Once you are enrolled in the Champion Program, the tablet travels to you through
a set sequence, and each step sends you a message. Merchants ask this as *tablet
kahan pahuncha*. Your most recent message is always the answer.

## The steps from enrolment to live

1. **Enrolled** — you are in the program and the tablet is being prepared.
2. **Dispatched** — it has left, and you are given a tracking reference and an
   expected arrival.
3. **Out for delivery** — it arrives today. Keep your identification ready,
   because delivery is verified.
4. **Delivered** — it is with you. The team contacts you to arrange installation.
5. **Installed and activated** — it is set up and working. You can take payments
   and run offers on it.
6. **Live** — you are fully on the program and can track your rewards in the app.

There may also be a short **training** session arranged between installation and
going live.

## Delivered is not the same as ready

This is the step that surprises people. When the tablet is **delivered**, it is
in your hands but not yet set up — installation is a separate visit that LessPay
arranges with you afterwards.

A tablet sitting on your counter that does nothing yet is expected at that
point, not a fault.

## Keep your identification ready on delivery day

The out-for-delivery message asks you to have your identification available.
Delivery is verified against it, so not having it to hand is the usual reason a
delivery has to be attempted again.

## What if the tablet has not arrived

- **Check the last message you received.** It names the stage, and dispatch
  messages carry a tracking reference and an expected date.
- **Delivered but nothing since** — installation is arranged separately and the
  team contacts you. That contact is the next step, not another delivery.
- **Nothing since enrolment** — the tablet has not been dispatched yet.

## Hinglish mein

Tablet ka safar tay hai aur har stage par message aata hai. Enrolled matlab
program mein aa gaye. Dispatched matlab tablet nikal gaya aur tracking mil jati
hai. Out for delivery matlab aaj hi aayega, apna pehchaan patra taiyar rakhiye.
Delivered matlab mil gaya, lekin abhi chalu nahi hua — install karne team alag
se aati hai. Install hone ke baad hi aap us par payment le sakte hain aur phir
aap live ho jate hain.
