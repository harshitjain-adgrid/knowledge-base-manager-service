# What Promotions Are

A promotion is paid reach. Your offers are free to run — a promotion is you
paying to put your shop or your offer in front of more customers than would
find it on their own.

## The five channels

| Channel | What it does |
|---|---|
| Featured | Your shop appears in a card on the customer app's home — popular nearby, new nearby, or great deals |
| Poster | A banner or image on one of the customer app's screens |
| WhatsApp | A WhatsApp message goes out to customers |
| Text | An SMS goes out to customers |
| Notification | A push notification goes out to customers |

## Two ways of reaching people

The five channels split into two kinds, and they behave differently:

- **Placements** — featured and posters. Your shop **occupies a slot** in the
  app for a period. You pay for time.
- **Pushes** — WhatsApp, text and notification. A message **goes out to people**
  once. You pay for how many people.

That difference is why a placement is priced per day and a push is not. Paying
for a seven-day WhatsApp blast would make no sense; it is sent once.

## Where posters can appear

A poster promotion is bought against a specific screen in the customer app —
the home banner, the home feed, a list banner, the payment screen, a story, or
a popup. Each one is a different template with its own price.

## Promotions and offers are separate

A promotion can point at one of your offers, or just at your shop. They are not
the same thing:

- An **offer** is the discount or deal itself. Free to run.
- A **promotion** is paying to show it to more people. Costs money.

Running a promotion without a good offer behind it usually gets you views
without visits.

## What if a promotion does not bring customers

A promotion buys attention, not visits. The usual reasons it does not convert:

- **There was no offer behind it.** People saw the shop and had no reason to
  choose it over the one next door.
- **The reach was too small or too far.** A tight radius in a quiet area reaches
  very few people.
- **The channel did not suit the message.** A one-time push is good for
  something happening today; a placement is better for lasting visibility.

## Hinglish mein

Promotion matlab paise dekar zyada logon tak pahunchna. Aapke offer chalane ka
koi kharcha nahi hai, lekin unhein zyada logon ko dikhane ke liye paisa lagta
hai. Paanch channel hote hain: featured, poster, WhatsApp, SMS aur
notification. Inmein do tarah ke hain — placement matlab featured aur poster,
jismein aapki dukaan app mein kuch din ke liye jagah leti hai aur aap din ke
hisaab se paisa dete hain. Push matlab WhatsApp, SMS aur notification, jo ek
baar jaate hain aur kitne logon ko gaye uske hisaab se paisa lagta hai. Offer
aur promotion alag hain: offer chhoot hai, promotion uska prachar hai.
