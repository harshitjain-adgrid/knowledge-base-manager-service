# Running a Promotion Campaign

Buying a promotion means choosing a channel, setting who and how long, paying,
and letting it run.

## The steps to run a campaign

1. Open **Promotions** and start a new campaign.
2. Pick the **channel** — featured, poster, WhatsApp, text or notification.
3. For a poster, pick the **template**, which is the screen it appears on.
4. Choose the **offer** you want it to point at, or leave it pointing at your
   shop.
5. Set **who sees it** — the radius around your shop and how many people.
6. Set **how long** it runs. The cost updates as you change these, so you can
   see what a wider radius or an extra day actually costs before committing.
7. Pay. The campaign goes live once payment completes.

## What happens after you pay

A campaign moves through three states:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Draft | You are still setting it up. Nothing is charged. |
| Awaiting payment | You have confirmed it and payment is in progress. |
| Active | Paid for and running. |

If a payment fails, the campaign goes back to draft rather than disappearing.
Your settings are still there and you can try again.

## Cancelling

Cancelling switches a campaign off. It stays in your list with its history — how
many people it reached, what it cost — rather than vanishing. Nothing in LessPay
deletes your business records outright.

## What if the payment fails

Your campaign returns to draft with everything you entered intact. Try again
from the same campaign rather than building a new one.

If the price has changed since you set it up, you will be asked to accept the
new price before paying.

## What if a campaign is not getting views

- **The radius may be too small.** One kilometre in a quiet area reaches very
  few people.
- **The audience may be set low.** A hundred people is the minimum, and it is a
  small number.
- **The offer behind it may be weak.** A promotion buys attention; the offer is
  what converts it.

## Hinglish mein

Promotion chalane ke liye Promotions kholein aur naya campaign shuru karein.
Pehle channel chunein — featured, poster, WhatsApp, SMS ya notification. Poster
ho to template chunein. Phir tay karein ki campaign aapke kisi offer ki taraf
ishara karega ya sirf dukaan ki. Uske baad radius aur kitne log dekhenge yeh
set karein, aur kitne din chalega. Jaise jaise aap badlenge, kharcha upar
dikhta rahega. Aakhir mein payment karein aur campaign chalu ho jayega. Payment
fail ho jaye to campaign draft mein wapas aa jata hai, aapki saari setting
waisi hi rehti hai aur aap dobara koshish kar sakte hain.
