# Why Your Customers Get Messages

When you run a deal, customers can be messaged about it — that it is ending, or
that it is in its final hours. These are promotional messages, and they follow
stricter rules than the messages you receive about your own account.

## Offer messages need the customer's agreement first

A message telling a customer about a deal is promotional, not transactional.
That means it can only go to customers who have agreed to receive that kind of
message, and every one of them carries a way to stop.

This is not a LessPay preference. Promotional messaging is governed by the rules
of the channels themselves, and a business that ignores them loses the ability
to message at all.

## STOP is permanent and immediate

Every promotional message tells the customer they can reply **STOP**. When they
do, they stop receiving that kind of message.

It is not a pause and it is not per-offer. Treat every send as spending
something that does not come back: a customer who opts out is out for your
future offers too.

## Why blasting everyone hurts you specifically

Messaging runs on shared infrastructure. When messages go to people who did not
want them, the people who receive them mark them as unwanted — and the damage
lands on the sending reputation that **every merchant on LessPay shares**.

The practical effect is that a merchant who messages indiscriminately makes
delivery worse for merchants who do not. Sending to customers likely to care is
not politeness; it is what keeps the channel working.

## What customers are told about

- A deal of yours is **ending soon**.
- A deal is in its **final hours**.
- A deal has **ended**.

All three are about timing, which is what makes them worth sending at all — a
message about something the customer can still act on.

## What if a customer says they are getting too many messages

They can opt out themselves by replying STOP to any of them, and that is the
honest answer to give. There is no way to reverse it on their behalf, and
sending more to a customer who has said this is what produces the opt-out.

## Not this — customers are not getting your account messages

Messages about your KYC, PIN, payments and settlements go to **you** only.
Customers never receive anything about your account, your money or your
verification. For what you receive, see "Messages LessPay Sends You".

## Hinglish mein

Jab aap deal chalate hain to customer ko message ja sakta hai ki offer khatam
hone wala hai. Ye promotional message hain, isliye niyam sakht hain: sirf un
customer ko ja sakta hai jinhone haan kaha ho, aur har message mein STOP karne
ka tareeka hota hai. Ek baar customer STOP kar de to aage ke offer ke message
bhi band ho jate hain. Sabko bina soche message bhejne se sabhi merchant ki
delivery kharab hoti hai, kyunki infrastructure sabka ek hi hai.
