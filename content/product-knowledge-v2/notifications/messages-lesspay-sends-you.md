# Messages LessPay Sends You

LessPay reaches you over SMS and WhatsApp. Every message is triggered by
something that actually happened on your account — none of it is a broadcast, so
a message arriving means a state on your account changed or is waiting on you.

## What you get messages about

- **Signing in** — the one-time password, or the WhatsApp login link.
- **Your PIN** — every time it is set or changed.
- **KYC** — when it is under review, approved, or rejected.
- **Payments** — received, pending, failed, refunded.
- **Settlements** — being processed, credited, or on hold.
- **Security** — anything that looks like someone else acting on your account.
- **Rewards** — referral credits, and warnings before a balance expires.
- **Champion Program** — each stage of your tablet's journey, if you are enrolled.

## A reminder means a step is genuinely outstanding

Reminders are not scheduled nags. Each one fires only when the step before it is
finished and the step it names is not, and it stops the moment you do it.

So a reminder to complete KYC means KYC is actually incomplete. Doing the thing
is what makes it stop; there is nothing to mute.

## The two messages worth never ignoring

Most messages are informational. Two are not:

- **A PIN was set or changed** and you did not do it.
- **A sign-in happened** on a device or at a time you do not recognise.

Either one means somebody else may be in your account. Both arrive with a way to
act on them, and acting immediately is the right response.

## Why some messages arrive on WhatsApp and some on SMS

Both channels are used, and which one carries what is decided by the rules each
channel operates under rather than by preference. One-time passwords and login
links are handled strictly, and never carry promotional content or extra links —
if a message claiming to be a LessPay login also tries to sell you something, it
is not from LessPay.

## Not this — these are not the messages your customers get

The messages here are the ones **you** receive about your own account. Your
customers get a different set, about offers and their coins, and those follow
different rules including consent and opting out. For that, see "Why Your
Customers Get Messages".

## Hinglish mein

LessPay aapko SMS aur WhatsApp par message bhejta hai, aur har message kisi asli
ghatna par aata hai — koi random broadcast nahi hota. Login OTP, PIN badalne,
KYC, payment, settlement, reward aur Champion Program ke stage — in sab par
message aata hai. Reminder tabhi aata hai jab koi kaam sach mein baaki ho, aur
kaam hote hi apne aap band ho jata hai. Do message kabhi ignore mat kariye: PIN
badalne ka aur anjaan device se login ka.
