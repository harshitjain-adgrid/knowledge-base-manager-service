# What Chotu Can Do for You

Chotu is the assistant inside LessPay Business. You can ask it questions about
your shop and about how the app works, and you can ask it to prepare things for
you. Ask in Hindi, English, or a mix — it understands all three.

## Questions Chotu answers on its own

Anything you can see in the app, Chotu can read and explain without asking your
permission first:

- How much you took today, this week or this month
- Which payments came in, and what a particular payment was for
- When your next settlement is due and how much is pending
- What offers you have running, how many people have used them
- What is in your wallet and when any of it expires
- How anything in the app works

These are all read-only, so Chotu just answers.

## Things Chotu can prepare for you

Chotu can put together a draft and hand it to you:

- A discount or a deal, filled in with the details you described
- A promotion campaign, with the cost worked out
- Suggestions — for example spotting that a particular day is slow and proposing
  a happy-hours discount for it

A draft is not live. Nothing has been created until you confirm it.

## What Chotu will never do by itself

**Chotu cannot create, change or delete anything that touches money, offers or
your QR codes on its own.** Every one of those actions needs you to type your
PIN. The pattern is always the same:

1. You ask for something.
2. Chotu prepares it and shows you exactly what it will do.
3. You type your PIN to confirm.
4. Only then does it happen.

There is no setting that turns this off, and no way for Chotu to act while you
are away from the phone. That is deliberate — it is your money and your shop.

## Offers Chotu makes are marked

An offer that Chotu drafted carries a badge in your offers list, so you can
always tell at a glance which ones you made yourself and which ones came from a
suggestion. It behaves exactly like one you made by hand.

## What if Chotu says it cannot do something

Two common reasons:

- **Your shop is still being set up.** Chotu only offers money-related actions
  once your shop is fully active. Until then it can still answer questions.
- **The rules do not allow it.** For example, it will refuse to delete the QR
  code you are currently using, rather than walking you through the PIN and
  then failing.

## Hinglish mein

Chotu LessPay Business ke andar ka sahayak hai. Aap Hindi, English ya dono mila
kar puch sakte hain. Jo cheezein sirf padhni hain — aaj kitna kamaya, agla
settlement kab hai, kaunse offer chal rahe hain, wallet mein kya hai — wo Chotu
seedha bata deta hai, kisi permission ki zarurat nahi. Jo cheezein banani hain,
jaise discount, deal ya promotion, unka Chotu sirf draft banata hai aur aapko
dikhata hai. Kuch bhi asli tab tak nahi hota jab tak aap PIN daal kar confirm
na karein. Chotu apne aap paisa, offer ya QR se juda koi kaam kabhi nahi karta.
