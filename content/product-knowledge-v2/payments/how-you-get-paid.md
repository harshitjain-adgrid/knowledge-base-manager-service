# How You Get Paid

Customers pay you by UPI, by scanning a QR code at your counter. The money goes
to your bank account — LessPay does not hold it in between.

## What happens when a customer pays

The customer scans your QR. Their app recognises your shop and shows every offer
they qualify for. They pick one, the app works out what they owe after the
discount, and they pay by UPI from their own bank or UPI app. The payment then
appears in your payments list.

You do not apply the discount by hand. The app has already checked the customer
against the rules you set when you made the offer.

## Two kinds of QR, both counted

Your payments are split by where they came from:

- **LessPay QR** — the QR code LessPay gave you. Offers apply on these payments.
- **Other QR** — payments taken on a QR from another provider.

Both show in your payments, so your day's total is the whole picture rather than
just the LessPay part.

## Payment status

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Received | The payment succeeded. The money is yours, awaiting settlement |
| Pending | Awaiting confirmation. No action is needed from you |
| Failed | It did not go through; nothing was taken |
| Refunded | It was returned to the customer |

## A receipt for every payment

Opening a payment shows what came in and what you actually keep: the amount the
customer paid, the amount after fees, how they paid, and the payer's name.

It also carries the **UTR** — the bank's reference number for the transfer. The
UTR is empty until the payment is settled to your bank, which is normal and not
a sign of a problem.

## What if a payment is missing

- **The customer paid on another provider's QR.** Switch the source filter — it
  will be under other QR, not LessPay QR.
- **It is still pending.** Pending payments have not confirmed yet. Give it a
  few minutes.
- **You are looking at the wrong day.** The day boundary is Indian time. A
  payment taken close to midnight belongs to the day the app says, not the day
  it felt like.

## Not this

This is the payment — the customer's money leaving their account. The money
arriving in **your bank account** is a separate event on its own schedule, and
that is a settlement rather than a payment.

## Hinglish mein

Customer aapka QR scan karta hai, uske app mein aapki dukaan aur uske liye
available offer dikhte hain, wo ek chunta hai aur UPI se paisa bhej deta hai.
Discount aapko haath se lagana nahi padta — app pehle hi hisaab kar deta hai.
Paisa seedha aapke UPI account mein jata hai, LessPay beech mein nahi rakhta.
Payment ki chaar halat hoti hain: Success matlab paisa aa gaya, Pending matlab
abhi confirm nahi hua, Failed matlab hua hi nahi, Refunded matlab wapas chala
gaya. Har payment par UTR bhi hota hai — yeh bank ka reference number hai aur
settlement hone tak khali rehta hai, yeh koi galti nahi hai.
