# When Your Money Reaches Your Bank

A payment and a settlement are two different events. The payment is the customer
paying you. The settlement is that money arriving in your bank account.

## The gap is normal

Money does not move to your bank the instant a customer pays. It is collected
and transferred on a schedule, which your settlements screen shows you.

Your settlements summary tells you three things: how much is pending, when the
next transfer goes out, and which bank account it is going to. Read the schedule
on that screen rather than assuming a cycle — it is shown to you there.

## Settlement status

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Pending / processing | The settlement batch has started. The money is on its way. |
| Completed | The funds are credited to your bank. The message names the account and a UTR. |
| Failed / on hold | It could not be settled. A hold carries a reason and needs you to act on it. |

A settlement that is processing has no **UTR** yet — the bank's reference number
only exists once the transfer completes. An empty UTR on a processing settlement
is expected, not a fault.

The third state covers two situations that feel different to you. A transfer the
bank rejected is usually wrong or closed account details. A settlement LessPay
has held always carries a reason, and that reason is the thing to act on.

## Your bank account

Your settlements go to whichever of your bank accounts is marked active. You can
see all your accounts with their numbers partly hidden, and switch which one is
active.

**Switching accounts affects future payouts only.** A settlement already on its
way continues to the account it was sent to. If you change your account today,
today's pending amount may still land in the old one.

## What if a settlement has not arrived

- **Check the status.** Processing means it is with the bank and still moving.
- **Check the schedule.** If your settlements go out daily in the morning,
  money taken this afternoon goes tomorrow, not tonight.
- **Check which account is active.** A recently changed account is the usual
  reason money arrives somewhere unexpected.
- **Failed settlements** normally mean the bank rejected the transfer — usually
  wrong or closed account details.

## Not this

A settlement that has been deliberately **held** by LessPay is a different case
from one that is simply still processing or has failed at the bank. A hold
carries a reason and needs you to act on that reason.

## Hinglish mein

Payment aur settlement do alag cheezein hain. Payment matlab customer ne aapko
paisa diya. Settlement matlab wo paisa aapke bank account mein pahunchna. Beech
mein waqt lagta hai aur yeh normal hai — paisa ek schedule par bank bheja jata
hai, jo aapki settlement screen par likha hota hai. Teen halat hoti hain:
Success matlab bank mein aa gaya, Processing matlab bank ke paas hai aur chal
raha hai, Failed matlab transfer nahi hua. Processing wale par UTR khali rehta
hai kyunki UTR tabhi banta hai jab transfer poora ho jaye. Bank account badalne
par sirf aage wale payout badalte hain, jo pehle se nikal chuka hai wo purane
account mein hi jayega.
