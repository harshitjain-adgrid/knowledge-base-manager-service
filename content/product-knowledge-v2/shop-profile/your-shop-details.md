# Your Shop Name, Category and Address

These three things decide how your shop appears to customers and where it turns
up when they search.

## The steps to change your shop details

1. Open your **shop profile** in LessPay Business.
2. Pick the field you want to change — the shop's name, its category, or its
   address.
3. Enter the new value. If you are changing the category, choose the
   sub-category again as well; the old one does not carry over.
4. Save, and confirm with your **MPIN**.
5. Check your shop page afterwards — what you changed is what customers now see.

## Your name and your shop's name are different

LessPay keeps two names, and they are not interchangeable:

- **Your name** — the owner's personal name, first and last. This is who the
  account belongs to.
- **Your shop's name** — the business name, for example *Sharma General Store*.
  This is what customers see at the top of your shop page.

Customers never see your personal name. If your shop page is showing the wrong
name, it is the business name you want to change, not your own.

## Category and sub-category

Every shop picks a category and then a sub-category inside it — for example
*Food and Beverages* then *Bakery*.

This is not just a label. Customers filter the list of shops by category, so a
shop in the wrong category is invisible to people looking for exactly what it
sells. Changing the category also changes which service modes and keywords you
are offered.

## Address

Your address decides which customers see you. The customer app shows shops
within a distance the customer chooses, so the address needs to be where your
shop actually is, not a home or billing address.

## What if your shop details are wrong

All three can be changed from your shop profile at any time. Changing your
category moves your shop into a different part of the customer app's listings,
so check the sub-category too — the old sub-category does not carry over to a
new category.

Changes to your profile are confirmed with your MPIN.

## Not this — your shop's address is not your payment address

Your shop's **address** is the street location customers see on your page and
use to find you. It decides which customers your shop is shown to, and you
change it from your shop profile.

Your **UPI ID** — the `name@bank` behind your QR code — is also called a
payment address, and it is a different field entirely. It decides where your
money lands, not where your shop is. Changing one never touches the other, and
the app refuses them for different reasons: a UPI ID is refused when the part
after the `@` is wrong or the provider is not supported, while a shop address
is simply text on your profile. For the payment side, see "Your UPI QR Code".

## Hinglish mein

Aapki dukaan ka naam, category aur address teeno milkar tay karte hain ki
customer ko aap kaise dikhte hain. Shop profile kholein, jo badalna hai wo
chunein, nayi value daalein aur MPIN se confirm karein. Dhyan rahe ki aapka
apna naam aur dukaan ka naam do alag cheezein hain — customer ko sirf dukaan
ka naam dikhta hai, aapka personal naam kabhi nahi. Category badalne par
sub-category dobara chunni padti hai, purani apne aap nahi aati. Address wahi
daalein jahan dukaan sach mein hai, ghar ya billing ka pata nahi, kyunki
customer app doori ke hisaab se dukaan dikhata hai.
