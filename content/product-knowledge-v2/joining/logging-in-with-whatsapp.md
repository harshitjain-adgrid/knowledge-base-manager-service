# Logging In With WhatsApp

Besides the usual mobile number and one-time password, you can log in through
WhatsApp. It suits merchants who already live in WhatsApp and would rather not
wait for an SMS. Merchants ask for this as *WhatsApp se login*.

## The steps to log in with WhatsApp

1. Tap **Login via WhatsApp** in the app.
2. WhatsApp opens with a message already written, addressed to the LessPay
   business number. Do not edit it.
3. **Send** that message.
4. LessPay replies in the same chat with a secure login link.
5. **Tap the link.** You are logged in.

## Do not edit the pre-filled message

The message that appears is the trigger. It is matched exactly, and it carries a
short one-time code that ties the message to your login attempt. Changing the
wording, or typing your own message instead, means nothing comes back.

If you have already edited it, close WhatsApp and start again from the app.

## The link is deliberately short-lived

The login link LessPay sends back is single-use, expires after a few minutes,
and works only on the device that asked for it. That is what stops a forwarded
message becoming a way into your account.

Practically, this means: use the link on the phone you tapped the button on, and
use it straight away.

## What if the link does not work

- **It expired.** Ask again from the app and a fresh link is sent. Old links do
  not come back to life.
- **You opened it on a different phone.** The link is bound to the device that
  requested it. Start again on that device.
- **You already used it.** Each link works once.
- **Nothing arrived at all.** The pre-filled message was probably edited or not
  actually sent.

## A confirmation follows a successful login

Once you are in, you get a message confirming the device and time you signed in
on. If one of those arrives and it was not you, treat it as a warning and change
your PIN.

## Not this — a login link is not your PIN

The WhatsApp link gets you *into* the app. Your **PIN** authorises actions once
you are in, such as payments and settlements. Receiving a login link does not
change or reset your PIN, and being logged in does not let anyone act without
it. For the PIN itself, see "Your PIN and What It Authorises".

## Hinglish mein

WhatsApp se login karne ke liye app mein Login via WhatsApp par tap kariye.
WhatsApp khulega aur ek message pehle se likha hoga — usse bina badle bhej
dijiye. LessPay usi chat mein ek login link bhejta hai, us par tap karte hi
login ho jata hai. Ye link sirf ek baar chalta hai, kuch hi minute chalta hai,
aur usi phone par chalta hai jahan se maanga tha. Link purana ho jaye to app se
dobara maang lijiye.
