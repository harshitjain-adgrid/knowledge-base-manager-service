# How You Join LessPay

Joining LessPay is four steps in a fixed order, and each one opens the next.
Merchants ask about this as *account kaise banaye* or *shuru kaise karein*. You
are considered onboarded the moment you log in for the first time; the rest is
what turns that account into one that can take money.

## The steps to join

1. **Log in** with your mobile number and the one-time password you receive, or
   through WhatsApp.
2. **Set your PIN.** One PIN, which you will reuse to authorise actions later.
3. **Complete KYC.** This is the identity check on your business.
4. **Start accepting payments** and running offers.

Each step unlocks the one after it. There is no way to skip ahead — a payment
screen opened before KYC is approved sends you back to finish verification.

## Why the order is fixed

The PIN comes before KYC because KYC is a sensitive action and the PIN is what
authorises sensitive actions. KYC comes before payments because LessPay cannot
move money for a business it has not verified.

## You are prompted one step at a time

You will not be asked for everything at once. Each prompt fires only when the
step before it is finished, and stops once you have done it.

If you are being reminded about something, it is because that step is genuinely
outstanding — the reminders are driven by your actual state, not sent on a
schedule.

## What if you are stuck between steps

- **Logged in but nothing else works** — your PIN is probably not set yet.
- **PIN set and payments still closed** — KYC has not been submitted or has not
  been approved.
- **Reminders keep arriving** — the step they name is not finished. They stop on
  their own once it is.

## Hinglish mein

LessPay join karne ke char step hain aur ye ek fixed order mein hote hain.
Pehle mobile number aur OTP se ya WhatsApp se login karna hota hai. Uske baad
ek PIN set karna hota hai, wahi PIN aage har zaroori kaam mein lagta hai. Phir
KYC yaani business ka verification karna padta hai. KYC approve hone ke baad hi
payment lena aur offer chalana shuru hota hai. Har step pura hone par hi agla
step khulta hai.
