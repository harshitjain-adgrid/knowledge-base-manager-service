# When a Settlement Is On Hold

A settlement is the transfer of money you have collected into your bank account.
Most of the time it just happens. When it does not, it lands in one of three
states, and the one that needs you is **on hold**.

## The steps to work out where your money is

1. Read the most recent settlement message you received. It names the state.
2. If it says **processing**, the batch has started and is with the bank.
3. If it says **completed**, look for the account it names and the **UTR** — the
   bank's reference for the transfer.
4. If it says **on hold**, read the reason it gives.
5. Contact support with that reason if it is not something you can fix yourself.

## The three states

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Processing | The settlement batch has started. Money is on its way. |
| Completed | Credited to your bank. The message names the account and a UTR. |
| On hold | It could not be settled, with a reason. It needs attention. |

## Processing is not a problem

A settlement that is processing is working. There is no UTR yet because the
bank's reference only exists once the transfer completes, and an empty UTR at
this stage is expected rather than a fault.

## On hold always carries a reason

An on-hold settlement is not silent. It tells you why, and it points you at
support. That reason is the useful thing — carrying it into a support
conversation is what makes the conversation short.

## Nothing settles before KYC is approved

Worth ruling out first if you have never received a settlement at all. Money
movement is gated on KYC, so an account that is not yet verified has nothing to
settle regardless of what customers have paid.

## What if the money still has not arrived

- **Check the state**, not the calendar. Processing means it is moving.
- **Check the account named** in the completed message. It may not be the one
  you expected if you changed accounts recently.
- **Check whether KYC is approved.** Before that, settlements do not run.
- **Read the hold reason.** It is the only thing that explains an on-hold
  settlement, and it does not clear itself.

## Hinglish mein

Settlement matlab jama hua paisa aapke bank account mein bhejna. Teen state
hoti hain: processing matlab bhejna shuru ho gaya hai, completed matlab bank
mein aa gaya aur message mein account aur UTR number hota hai, aur on hold
matlab ruk gaya hai aur uski wajah message mein likhi hoti hai. Processing mein
UTR nahi hota, ye normal hai. Agar aaj tak ek bhi settlement nahi aaya to pehle
dekhiye ki KYC approve hua hai ya nahi — bina KYC ke paisa nahi jata.
