# Offer Status and Validity

Every offer in your list carries a status, and that status is what decides
whether customers can actually use it right now.

## What each status means

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Active | Running now. Customers can use it. |
| Scheduled | Set up, but its start date has not arrived yet. |
| Inactive | Switched off. It stays in your list with its history. |
| Draft | Started and not finished. Not visible to anyone. |
| Expired | Its end date has passed. |

An offer in your list is not necessarily an offer customers can see. Check the
status before assuming something is running.

## Setting when it runs

An offer either runs for a period or runs until you stop it.

- **With an end date** — you set when it starts and when it ends. Choose this
  for anything tied to a festival, a weekend or a season.
- **Without an end date** — it runs until you switch it off. Choose this for a
  standing offer you do not want to keep renewing.

If you start it immediately, the start time is stamped as the moment you
created it.

## What your offers list tells you

Alongside the status, each offer shows how it is doing:

- **Views** — how many customers have seen it.
- **Redemptions** — how many times it has actually been used.
- **Expires in** — how many days are left, when there is an end date.

Views without redemptions usually means the offer is being seen but the terms
are not tempting, or a minimum bill is set higher than a typical bill.

## What if an offer expired and you want it back

An expired offer cannot be restarted — its dates are in the past. Create a new
one with the dates you want. The old one stays in your list with its numbers, so
you can compare how the two performed.

## Hinglish mein

Har offer ka ek status hota hai aur wahi tay karta hai ki customer use kar
sakta hai ya nahi. Active matlab abhi chal raha hai. Scheduled matlab bana to
hai lekin shuru hone ki date nahi aayi. Inactive matlab aapne band kar diya.
Draft matlab adhoora reh gaya. Expired matlab date nikal gayi. Offer banate
waqt ya to shuru aur khatam hone ki date dein, ya bina end date ke chalayein
jo tab tak chalta hai jab tak aap band na karein. Expired offer dobara chalu
nahi hota — naya banana padta hai, purana apne numbers ke saath list mein rehta
hai.
