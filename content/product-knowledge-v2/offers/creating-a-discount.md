# Creating a Discount

A discount takes money off the customer's bill — either a percentage or a fixed
amount. Merchants call it *chhoot* or simply *off*. Creating one is confirmed
with your MPIN.

## The steps to create a discount

1. Open **Offers** in LessPay Business and choose to create a new offer.
2. Pick **discount** rather than deal — a discount changes what the customer
   pays, a deal changes what they get.
3. Choose **percentage or flat**, then enter the amount: the percent off, or the
   rupees off.
4. Add any optional limits you want — a maximum saving, a minimum bill or an
   Nth-payment trigger, and happy hours.
5. Name the discount and decide whether it shows on your shop profile.
6. Set when it runs, then confirm with your **MPIN**.

## What you must decide

| Setting | Required | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Yes | What the offer is called, for example *Weekend 20% off* |
| Percentage or flat | Yes | Percentage off the bill, or a fixed rupee amount |
| Amount | Yes | The percentage, or the rupees off |
| Show on shop profile | Yes | Whether customers browsing your page can see it |

## Optional settings that shape who gets it

- **Maximum saving.** A cap on how much a percentage discount can take off. 20%
  off with a ₹100 cap means a ₹1,000 bill still only saves ₹100.
- **Minimum bill.** The discount only applies above a bill amount you set.
- **Every Nth payment.** Instead of a minimum bill, the discount applies on a
  customer's third payment, or fifth, or whichever number you choose.
- **Happy hours.** A start and end time, so the discount only applies between
  them. Both times are needed — one alone is not enough.
- **Weekends.** Whether the happy hours also apply on weekends. This one only
  matters if you have set happy hours.

## Minimum bill and Nth payment are alternatives

A discount triggers on **either** the bill amount **or** the payment count, not
both. If you set a minimum bill, you are not also counting payments; if you are
counting payments, the bill size does not matter.

Decide which lever you want before you start: *"spend more"* or *"come back
more often"*.

## What happens after you confirm

The discount is created and takes effect according to its validity. Customers
who qualify see it in their app when they scan your QR, and the saving is worked
out for them automatically. You never apply it by hand at the counter.

## What if the discount is not applying

The usual reasons, in the order worth checking:

- The bill is **below the minimum** you set.
- It is **outside the happy hours** window.
- The customer already used it as many times as you allowed.
- A different offer of yours saved them more, so that one applied instead — only
  one offer applies to a payment.

## Not this

This is how a discount is **set up**. Choosing between a discount and a deal in
the first place is a separate question, and so is who is allowed to use an offer
and how many times.

## Hinglish mein

Discount matlab bill ka paisa kam hota hai — ya to percent mein ya flat rupaye
mein. Banane ke liye Offers mein naya offer chunein, discount select karein,
percentage ya flat chunein aur amount daalein. Chahein to minimum bill, maximum
saving, happy hours ya har Nth payment jaisi condition laga sakte hain. Dhyan
rahe: minimum bill aur Nth payment dono ek saath nahi chalte, ek hi chunna hota
hai. Naam dein, shop profile par dikhana hai ya nahi tay karein, aur MPIN daal
kar confirm karein.
