# Deals and Discounts — the Difference

Everything you run to attract customers is an **offer**. There are exactly two
kinds, and which one you want depends on a single question: does the customer
pay less, or do they get more?

## A discount changes what they pay

A discount takes money off the bill. Two shapes:

- **Percentage** — 20% off.
- **Flat** — ₹100 off.

Use a discount when you want the bill to come down. It is the simplest kind to
set up and the easiest for a customer to understand at the counter.

## A deal changes what they get

A deal gives the customer more goods for the same money. Three shapes:

- **Buy one get one** — buy a coffee, get a coffee.
- **Bundle** — three items together for a set price.
- **Free item** — a free dessert on any bill over ₹500.

Use a deal when you would rather move stock or push a particular item than cut
your margin across the board.

## Which one to choose

| You want to | Use |
|---|---|
| Bring the bill down for everyone | Discount, percentage |
| Push people over a spend threshold | Discount, flat, with a minimum bill |
| Fill quiet hours | Discount with happy hours |
| Move a specific item | Deal — free item or bundle |
| Clear stock that comes in pairs | Deal — buy one get one |

## Both are called offers

In your offers list, and in the customer app, both show up together as offers.
The difference is in how they are set up, not in where they live.

If you tell Chotu you want to "run an offer", it will ask which of the two you
mean, because the numbers you give for one do not work for the other.

## What if you pick the wrong one

A discount and a deal cannot be converted into each other. If you set up a
discount and realise a deal was what you wanted, switch the discount off and
make the deal instead.

Nothing is lost by doing that. Offers are never deleted outright, so the one you
switched off stays in your list with its record intact.

## Hinglish mein

LessPay par do tarah ke offer hote hain. Discount matlab bill ka paisa kam — ya
percent mein ya flat rupaye mein. Deal matlab paisa utna hi lekin saman zyada —
buy one get one, bundle ya kisi bill ke upar free item. Seedha sawal yeh hai ki
customer kam paisa de ya zyada saman le. Margin bachana ho to deal behtar hai,
bill neeche laana ho to discount. Dono aapki offers list mein saath dikhte hain,
bas banane ka tarika alag hai. Ek ko doosre mein badla nahi ja sakta.
