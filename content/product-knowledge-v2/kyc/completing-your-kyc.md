# Completing Your KYC

KYC is the identity check on your business, and it is the last step between
signing up and taking money. Merchants call it *verification* or *kyc ka kaam*.
You submit it once, LessPay reviews it, and your account becomes fully active.

## The steps to complete KYC

1. Finish the steps before it — log in, and set your PIN.
2. Open **KYC** from your account and start the verification.
3. Submit the documents the app asks you for.
4. Wait for the review. Your KYC moves to **Under review** at this point.
5. Watch for the result — you are notified whether it is approved or rejected.

You do not have to sit in the app while it is reviewed. The result reaches you
as a message.

## The four states

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Pending | You have set your PIN but not submitted KYC yet |
| Under review | Your documents are in and LessPay is checking them |
| Approved | Verified. Payments and settlements are open |
| Rejected | Verification failed, with a reason. You can re-submit |

## Pending is a state, not a delay

If your KYC says **Pending**, nothing is being reviewed — it means the documents
have not been submitted yet. This is worth checking first if you think you are
waiting on LessPay and nothing seems to be happening.

While you are in this state you will get occasional reminders, and those stop as
soon as you submit.

## What happens on approval

Approval is the moment your account becomes fully active. You are told
explicitly that you can now accept payments, and from then on your offers apply
to real payments and money settles to your bank.

## What if it is taking longer than you expect

- **Check the state first.** Pending means nothing has been submitted; only
  Under review means it is actually with LessPay.
- **Check for a rejection message.** A rejected KYC carries a reason and needs
  you to re-submit; it does not resolve on its own.
- **Documents must be readable.** A blurred or cropped photo of a document is
  the most common cause of a slow or failed check.

## Hinglish mein

KYC karne ke liye pehle login aur PIN ho jana chahiye, uske baad KYC section
mein jaakar documents submit karne hote hain. Submit karte hi status Under
review ho jata hai aur LessPay check karta hai. Char state hoti hain: Pending
matlab abhi tak documents diye hi nahi, Under review matlab check ho raha hai,
Approved matlab ho gaya aur payment chalu, Rejected matlab nahi hua aur dobara
bhejna padega. Result aapko message se mil jata hai.
