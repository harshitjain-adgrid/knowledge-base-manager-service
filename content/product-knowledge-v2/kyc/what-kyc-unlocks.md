# What KYC Unlocks

KYC stands for *Know Your Customer* — it is the identity check LessPay has to
complete on your business before money can move. Merchants usually call it
*verification* or just *kagaz ka kaam*. Until it is approved, you can look
around the app but you cannot take money or receive it.

## KYC is the gate to money movement

This is the single rule worth remembering: **every feature that touches money is
locked until KYC is approved.** That covers accepting payments from customers
and receiving settlements into your bank account.

It is not a soft limit or a smaller version of the feature. The feature is
closed, and opening a payment screen before approval shows you a prompt to
finish verification rather than letting you try and fail.

## What is open before KYC

You are not locked out of the app. Before KYC is approved you can still sign in,
set your PIN, and set up your shop — name, category, photos.

What you cannot do is take a payment or be paid out.

## What opens after approval

Once your KYC is approved, your account is fully active:

- Customers can pay you.
- Collected money settles to your registered bank account.
- Offers you run actually apply to real payments.

You are told when this happens rather than having to check — approval sends you
a confirmation that payments are now open.

## Why it works this way

Taking payments on behalf of a business is regulated. LessPay has to know who
the business is before it moves money for it, and a payment taken by an
unverified account could not be settled anywhere.

Doing the check once, up front, is what allows everything afterwards to be
immediate.

## Not this — KYC is not your PIN

Your **PIN** is a secret you choose, and it authorises individual actions each
time you take one. **KYC** is a one-time identity check on your business, done
by LessPay, that decides whether money features exist for you at all. Setting
your PIN does not verify you, and completing KYC does not replace your PIN. For
the PIN, see "Your PIN and What It Authorises".

## Hinglish mein

KYC matlab aapke business ki pehchaan ka verification. Jab tak KYC approve nahi
hota, tab tak na aap payment le sakte hain aur na hi paisa aapke bank mein
settle hota hai — paise wale saare feature band rehte hain. Login karna, PIN
lagana aur dukaan ki details bharna KYC se pehle bhi ho jata hai. KYC approve
hone ke baad account poora chalu ho jata hai aur aapko iski jaankari mil jati
hai.
