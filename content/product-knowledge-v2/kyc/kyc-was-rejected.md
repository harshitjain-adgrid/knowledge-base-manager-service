# When Your KYC Is Rejected

A rejected KYC means the verification did not pass and your account is still
waiting. It is not final — you can submit again. Merchants ask about this as
*kyc reject ho gaya* or *verification fail*.

## The steps to fix it

1. Open the rejection message or your KYC screen and read the **reason**. Every
   rejection carries one.
2. Fix what the reason names — usually a document that could not be read or did
   not match your business details.
3. Re-submit the KYC from the same screen.
4. Wait for the new review. Your state goes back to **Under review**.

## A rejection always comes with a reason

You are not left guessing. The rejection tells you what failed and gives you the
way back in to re-submit. Reading that reason before re-sending is the whole
job — re-submitting the same documents unchanged produces the same result.

## Payments stay locked until it passes

Rejection leaves you where you were before: money features are closed. Nothing
you had is taken away, and your shop details, PIN and any offers you set up are
untouched. What is missing is only the approval.

## Common reasons a check does not pass

- The photo of a document is **blurred, cropped or badly lit**, so the details
  cannot be read.
- The details on the document **do not match** what you entered about your
  business.
- The document is **not one of the accepted types**. The app tells you which
  ones it wants when you submit.

## What if it is rejected more than once

Read the reason each time — a second rejection is often for a different cause
than the first, and treating it as the same problem is what turns two attempts
into five. If the reason is not something you can act on, contact support with
the reason text rather than re-submitting again.

## Hinglish mein

KYC reject hone ka matlab hai verification pass nahi hua, lekin ye aakhri baat
nahi hai — dobara bhej sakte hain. Har rejection ke saath ek reason aata hai,
usko padhna sabse zaroori hai. Aksar wajah hoti hai document ki photo saaf nahi
hai ya business ki details match nahi kar rahi. Sudhaar karke dobara submit
karne par status wapas Under review ho jata hai. Jab tak approve nahi hota,
payment aur settlement band rehte hain.
