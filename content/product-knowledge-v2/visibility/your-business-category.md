# Your Business Category

Every shop on LessPay sits in a business category. It is how customers browsing
by type find you, and how the app knows what kind of shop you are. Merchants ask
this as *dukaan ka type* or *category kaunsi rakhein*.

## The steps to set your category

1. Open your shop profile.
2. Find the **category** setting.
3. Pick the one that matches what you mostly sell.
4. Save the change.
5. Check your shop page as a customer would see it.

## The categories

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Food & Beverages | Anything eaten or drunk — restaurants, cafés, sweet shops |
| Beauty & Wellness | Salons, spas, wellness services |
| Fashion & Lifestyle | Clothing, footwear, accessories, lifestyle goods |
| Grocery & General | Kirana shops, provisions, general stores |
| Medical & Pharmacy | Chemists and pharmacies |
| Electronics & Hardware | Electronics, electricals, hardware |
| Stalls & Kiosks | Small-format shops — a stall or a kiosk rather than a shopfront |
| Service & others | Anything that does not fit the above |

## Pick by what you mostly sell

Most shops sell more than one kind of thing, and the category is not a
restriction on what you can sell — it is a signal about where you belong when a
customer is browsing.

Choose the one that matches the majority of your business. A tea stall that also
sells cigarettes is Food & Beverages, not Service & others.

## Stalls and Kiosks is about format, not trade

Every other category describes *what* you sell. Stalls & Kiosks describes *how*
you sell — a small stand rather than a shop. If you run a food stall, either
could be argued.

Prefer the one describing your trade when a customer would search by what they
want to buy, which is most of the time.

## Service & others is a last resort

It exists so nothing is unclassifiable, not as a convenient default. A shop
filed there is harder for a browsing customer to find, because nobody browses
"others" looking for a specific thing.

## What if none of them fits

Pick the closest one rather than leaving it unset. An unset or wrongly broad
category is worse for discovery than an imperfect but specific one, because
category is one of the ways customers narrow down what they are looking at.

## Hinglish mein

Har dukaan ek business category mein aati hai, jisse customer type ke hisab se
dhoondh sake. Category wahi chuniye jo aap sabse zyada bechte hain — chai ki
dukaan Food & Beverages mein aayegi, kirana Grocery & General mein. Stalls &
Kiosks category dukaan ke format ke bare mein hai, saman ke bare mein nahi.
Service & others sirf tab chuniye jab aur koi option na bache, warna customer ko
dhoondhne mein dikkat hoti hai.
