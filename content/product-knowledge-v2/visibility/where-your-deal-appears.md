# Where Your Deal Appears

Running a deal puts it in the customer app. *Where* it appears is a separate
question, and it is something LessPay sells. Merchants ask this as *mera offer
upar kaise aayega*.

## Two levels of placement

Every deal gets a place in the customer app. There are two kinds:

- **Standard placement** — the default listing position. Free, and every deal
  gets it.
- **High-visibility placement** — a paid position, such as the home banner or
  the top of search results.

The paid tier is about position, not about the deal itself. The same deal in a
better position is seen by more people; the deal does not change.

## Paying does not change your offer

This is worth separating clearly. What you pay for is **where people see it**.
The discount, the deal type, who qualifies and how many times — none of that is
affected by placement.

A weak offer in the home banner is still a weak offer, seen by more people.

## Customers do not see it labelled as paid

A customer browsing the app is not shown which placements were paid for. The
paid tier is a merchant-facing arrangement.

That means paid placement buys attention, not credibility — customers judge the
offer they see, not the position it sits in.

## Where the paid tier puts you

High-visibility placement means the positions customers reach first: the banner
on the customer app's home screen, and the top of search results when they look
for what you sell.

## Not this — placement is not the offer

An **offer** — a deal or a discount — is the thing a customer receives, and it
is free to run. **Placement** is where that offer is shown, and the better
positions cost money. If your question is about what to give the customer rather
than about who sees it, see the offers documentation instead.

## Hinglish mein

Har deal customer app mein dikhta hai, lekin kahan dikhta hai ye alag baat hai.
Do tarah ki jagah hoti hai: normal listing jo free hai aur har deal ko milti
hai, aur paid jagah jaise home ka banner ya search mein sabse upar. Paise dene
se offer nahi badalta, sirf zyada logon tak pahunchta hai. Customer ko ye nahi
dikhta ki kis jagah ke paise diye gaye hain.
