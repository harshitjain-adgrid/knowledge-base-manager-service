# Credits and Your Wallet

**Credits** are the reward currency on your LessPay account. They are credited
to you for things you do on the platform — most commonly for referring another
shop — and they sit on your merchant account rather than in your bank.

## How credits reach your account

Credits arrive when something you did qualifies for a reward. Referring another
shop is the usual route: when the shop you referred completes their first
qualifying action, the reward is released to you.

You do not have to claim anything. The credit is added to your account and you
are told when it happens.

## You are told when credits arrive

A message names the reward and what earned it, so a balance never changes
without an explanation you can go back and read. If you were expecting credits
and no message came, the reward has not been released yet rather than having
been missed.

## Your balance is on your account

Your credit balance is shown on your account, along with the amount due to
expire next and the date it goes. The total is what you hold; the expiry figure
is the part of it with a deadline attached.

## Not this — credits are not coins

**Credits** belong to you, the shopkeeper, and are credited to your merchant
account. **Coins** are the customer's currency in their own app, earned and
spent by them. They are not the same thing and they are not interchangeable. A
customer paying you partly with coins takes nothing out of your credit balance,
and your credits cannot be spent in a customer's app.

## Hinglish mein

Credit aapke LessPay account par milne wala reward hai. Yeh aapko platform par
kiye gaye kaam ka inaam hota hai — sabse aam tareeka hai kisi doosri dukaan ko
refer karna. Jab wo dukaan apna pehla qualifying kaam poora karti hai, tab
aapka reward khul jata hai aur credit aapke account mein aa jata hai. Aapko
message aata hai ki kitna credit mila aur kis wajah se, isliye balance kabhi
bina wajah nahi badalta. Account par aapka total balance dikhta hai aur saath
mein wo hissa bhi jo agla expire hone wala hai. Credit aapka hai; coins
customer ke apne app ki cheez hai, dono alag hain.
