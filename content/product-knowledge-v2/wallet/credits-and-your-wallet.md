# Credits and Your Wallet

**Credits** are what you spend on promotions inside LessPay. You get them by
buying them, or as a benefit of a package you subscribe to.

## Two buckets, not one balance

Your wallet holds credits in two separate buckets, and they do not behave the
same way:

| Bucket | Where it comes from | Does it expire | What it can buy |
|---|---|---|---|
| Credit | Money you paid — top-ups and package credits | No | Any promotion channel |
| Rewards | Referrals, program grants, goodwill | Yes | LessPay's own channels only |

Your wallet screen shows a total, and the two buckets underneath it. The total
is what you have; the split is what you can do with it.

## Why rewards credit is more limited

Credit you paid for is yours to spend anywhere. Reward credit was given to you,
so it is restricted to LessPay's own promotion channels and it has an expiry
date attached.

That is worth knowing before planning a campaign around a large-looking balance:
part of it may not be spendable on the channel you had in mind.

## Perishable credit is used first

When you pay for a promotion, credit that is going to expire is spent before
credit that will not. You never lose credit that could have been used because
something permanent was spent ahead of it.

## Not this — credits are not coins

Credits belong to you, the shopkeeper, and buy promotions inside LessPay.
**Coins** are the customer's currency in their own app. They are not the same
thing, they are not interchangeable, and a customer spending coins takes nothing
out of your wallet.

## What if your balance looks wrong

- **Part of it may be reward credit** that cannot be used on the channel you
  are looking at.
- **Some of it may have expired.** Your wallet shows the amount that is due to
  expire and the date.
- **A package benefit may not have landed yet.** Monthly package credits arrive
  on the package's own schedule, not the day you ask.

## Hinglish mein

Credit wo cheez hai jisse aap LessPay ke andar promotion khareedte hain. Yeh
ya to aap paise dekar lete hain ya kisi package ke saath milta hai. Aapke
wallet mein do alag dabbe hote hain. Pehla Credit — jo aapne paise dekar liya,
kabhi expire nahi hota aur kisi bhi channel par lag jata hai. Doosra Rewards —
jo aapko inaam mein mila, iski expiry hoti hai aur yeh sirf LessPay ke apne
channel par chalta hai. Wallet par upar total dikhta hai aur neeche dono ka
hisaab. Jo credit pehle expire hone wala hai, kharch pehle wahi hota hai.
