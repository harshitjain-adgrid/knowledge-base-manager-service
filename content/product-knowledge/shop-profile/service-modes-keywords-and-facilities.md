# Service Modes, Keywords and Facilities

After your shop's name, category and address, LessPay asks for three more sets
of details. All three affect what customers see and how easily they find you.

## Service modes

Service modes are the ways a customer can buy from you — for example dine-in,
home delivery, or takeaway.

The list you are offered depends on your sub-category, because what makes sense
for a bakery is not what makes sense for a salon. Pick every mode you actually
offer; each one is a way for a customer to end up at your shop.

## Keywords

Keywords are what your shop is known for. There are two kinds:

- **Suggested keywords**, offered from a list that matches your sub-category.
- **Your own keywords**, which you type in yourself for anything the list does
  not cover.

Use the words your customers use. If people ask for *chhole bhature* and your
keywords only say *North Indian cuisine*, you are harder to find.

## Facilities

Facilities are the practical things about visiting your shop — parking, card
payment accepted, air conditioning, wheelchair access, and so on.

Unlike service modes, facilities come from a single list that is the same for
every kind of shop. A salon and a bakery choose from the same set.

## Everything is shown as a full list

When you open any of these three, the app shows you every available option with
the ones you have already picked marked. You are not adding to a hidden list —
you can see the whole thing and turn options on and off.

## What if the option you want is not listed

Service modes and facilities come from fixed lists and cannot be added to from
the app. Keywords are where you have freedom: add your own for anything the
suggested list misses.
