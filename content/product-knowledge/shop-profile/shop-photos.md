# Photos on Your Shop Page

Photos are the first thing a customer sees, and shops with real photos get
noticeably more first-time visits than shops without.

## What you can upload

| Slot | How many | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Profile photo | 1 | The small image next to your shop name |
| Cover photo | 1 | The wide image across the top of your shop page |
| Menu or catalogue | Up to 3 | Pages of your menu or product list |
| Shop photos | Up to 3 | The shop itself — inside, outside, the counter |
| Popular for | Up to 5 | Individual items you are known for |

## Popular for

*Popular for* is different from the other slots. Each entry is an item with both
a name and a picture — for example a photo of your samosa with the name written
against it.

These are the items customers see called out on your page, so use the things
people actually come to you for rather than your full range.

## Saving photos replaces the whole set

This is the one thing worth being careful about. When you save your photos, the
set you submit **replaces everything that was there**, rather than adding to it.

If you are updating one photo, make sure the others are still in the screen
before you save. If your *popular for* items disappear after an update, this is
almost always why.

## What if a photo will not upload

- **The picture is too large.** Try a photo taken at normal quality rather than
  the largest setting your phone offers.
- **The upload stops partway.** Photos upload directly from your phone, so a
  weak connection interrupts them. Try again on a better signal.
- **You have hit the limit.** Each slot has a fixed number. Remove one before
  adding another.
