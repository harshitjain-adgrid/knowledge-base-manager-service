# Your Shop Name, Category and Address

These three things decide how your shop appears to customers and where it turns
up when they search.

## Your name and your shop's name are different

LessPay keeps two names, and they are not interchangeable:

- **Your name** — the owner's personal name, first and last. This is who the
  account belongs to.
- **Your shop's name** — the business name, for example *Sharma General Store*.
  This is what customers see at the top of your shop page.

Customers never see your personal name. If your shop page is showing the wrong
name, it is the business name you want to change, not your own.

## Category and sub-category

Every shop picks a category and then a sub-category inside it — for example
*Food and Beverages* then *Bakery*.

This is not just a label. Customers filter the list of shops by category, so a
shop in the wrong category is invisible to people looking for exactly what it
sells.

## Address

Your address decides which customers see you. The customer app shows shops
within a distance the customer chooses, so the address needs to be where your
shop actually is, not a home or billing address.

## What if your shop details are wrong

All three can be changed from your shop profile at any time. Changing your
category moves your shop into a different part of the customer app's listings,
so check the sub-category too — the old sub-category does not carry over to a
new category.

Changes to your profile are confirmed with your MPIN.
