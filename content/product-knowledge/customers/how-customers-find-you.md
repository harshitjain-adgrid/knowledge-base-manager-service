# How Customers Find Your Shop

Customers using the LessPay app reach your shop in three ways, and each one
depends on something different about your setup.

## By looking around them

The customer app shows shops near where the customer is. Your position in that
depends on how close you are and how complete and appealing your listing is.

This is why your photos, your keywords and your service modes matter — they are
what make a nearby shop worth tapping on.

## By searching

A customer searching for something is matched against your shop name, what you
sell, and the keywords on your profile. A shop with thin keywords is harder to
find by search even when it is the closest one.

## By scanning your QR

A customer at your counter scans your QR. This does not depend on search or
distance at all — they are already with you. What matters here is that your
offers are set up, because the scan is where the customer sees them.

## Offers are what turn a listing into a visit

Being visible gets you looked at. An offer is what gets you visited. A customer
browsing nearby shops sees which ones have something on, and the app can list
shops by the deals they are running.

## What if you are not appearing for customers

- **Your profile may be incomplete.** A shop without photos or a category is
  hard to place and hard to trust.
- **Your keywords may be too narrow.** Customers search for what they want, not
  for your shop's name.
- **You may have no live offer.** Offer-led listings skip shops with nothing on.
- **You may be genuinely far** from where the customer is looking.
