# What Customers See About Your Shop

Knowing what appears on the customer's side helps you understand which parts of
your profile actually do work.

## Your shop page

A customer opening your shop sees your name, your photos, your category, where
you are, how you serve — dine-in, takeaway, delivery — and what facilities you
have. Below that are the offers you have running.

Everything on that page comes from your profile. Nothing is written for you, so
an empty field is an empty space on the customer's screen.

## Your offers, filtered to them

A customer does not see every offer you run — they see the ones **they qualify
for**. An offer aimed at new customers does not show to a regular, and one aimed
at lapsed customers does not show to someone who came last week.

This is deliberate. It means the customer only sees things they can actually
use, and you are not explaining why an offer did not apply.

## At the moment of paying

When a customer scans your QR, their app shows the offers they qualify for and
works out the discounted amount. They pick one, and pay the reduced sum.

You do not calculate anything or apply the discount by hand.

## Coins are theirs, not yours

Customers earn **coins** in their own app for using LessPay. Coins are the
customer's currency — they are not credits, they do not come out of your wallet,
and they are not something you manage.

## Offers Chotu made are marked

An offer created through Chotu carries a mark showing it came from Chotu. It
behaves exactly like one you made yourself; the mark is just provenance.

## What if a customer says your offer did not show

- **They may not qualify.** An offer aimed at new customers does not appear for
  someone who has paid you before.
- **They may have paid on another QR.** Offers only apply on payments made
  through your LessPay QR.
- **The offer may not be live.** A scheduled offer has not started yet, and an
  inactive one is switched off.
- **They may have hit the limit** on how many times one customer can use it.
