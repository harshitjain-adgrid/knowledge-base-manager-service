# What LessPay Does for Your Shop

LessPay puts your shop in front of people nearby, lets them pay you by UPI, and
gives them a discount you decide. You keep your own UPI account — LessPay never
holds your money.

There are two apps. You use **LessPay Business**. Your customers use the LessPay
customer app to find shops near them.

## What you get out of it

Three things, in the order most shopkeepers care about them:

- **You get found.** Your shop appears in the customer app for people searching
  or browsing nearby, with your photos, timings and whatever you are known for.
- **You get paid.** Customers scan your UPI QR in the shop and pay. The money
  goes to your bank account on the normal settlement schedule.
- **You bring people in.** You set discounts and deals that customers can see
  before they walk in, and you can pay to be shown to more people.

## How a customer actually pays you

The whole loop takes a few seconds at your counter:

1. The customer scans your LessPay QR.
2. Their app shows your shop and every offer they are eligible for right now.
3. They pick an offer, and the app works out what they owe after the discount.
4. They pay by UPI.

You do not have to apply the discount by hand or remember who qualifies. The app
checks it against the rules you set when you made the offer.

## LessPay never holds your money

Payments settle straight through to your bank account. LessPay is not a wallet
sitting in the middle holding your takings — it splits the payment at the moment
it happens.

That is why your settlement lands on a schedule rather than instantly: it is the
banking system moving it, not LessPay releasing it.

## What LessPay charges for

Using the shop profile, taking payments and running your own offers is not what
you pay for. LessPay makes money from:

- **Packages and programs** you subscribe to, which give you credits and other
  benefits every month.
- **Promotions** — paying to have your shop or offer shown to more customers
  inside the customer app.

## What if something is not working the way you expect

Almost everything in LessPay comes back to one of four things: your shop
profile, your offers, your QR, or your wallet. Working out which of the four a
problem belongs to usually points straight at the answer.

Chotu can also just be asked. Describing the problem in your own words —
English or Hinglish — is enough; you do not need to know what the feature is
called.
