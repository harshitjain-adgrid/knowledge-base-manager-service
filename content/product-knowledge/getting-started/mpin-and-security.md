# Your MPIN and What It Protects

Your MPIN is a 4-digit PIN you set while your shop is being set up. It is the
one thing standing between your account and anyone who picks up your unlocked
phone.

## Which actions ask for your MPIN

| Action | Needs MPIN |
|---|---|
| Unlocking the app | Yes |
| Creating a discount | Yes |
| Creating a deal | Yes |
| Deleting an offer | Yes |
| Adding, switching or deleting a QR code | Yes |
| Changing your profile | Yes |
| Looking at payments, settlements, wallet, offers | No |
| Asking Chotu a question | No |

The rule behind the list: **reading is free, changing money-related things is
not.**

## It asks every single time

There is no "stay unlocked for 10 minutes" and no way to authorise several
actions with one entry. Every sensitive action asks again, on its own.

That is inconvenient by design. It means a phone left on the counter cannot be
used to create offers or move your QR codes around.

## Your PIN never leaves your phone as you typed it

The PIN is scrambled on your own device before it is sent, using a key that
belongs only to that device. Nobody at LessPay can read it, and it is not stored
anywhere in a form that could be read back.

## What if you enter it wrong

The action is refused and nothing happens. Your offer is not half-created and no
money moves. You can try again.

## What if you forget it

Your MPIN can be set again through the app's PIN reset flow, which verifies you
by OTP on your registered mobile number first. Support cannot tell you what your
existing PIN is — nobody can read it.
