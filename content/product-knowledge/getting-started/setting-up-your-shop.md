# Setting Up Your Shop

Setting up happens in a fixed order. The app always opens on whichever step you
have not finished yet, so you cannot lose your place.

## Signing in

You sign in with your mobile number. LessPay sends a one-time password by SMS —
there is no password to remember and none to forget.

## The steps, in order

| Step | What it asks for |
|---|---|
| Your details | Your first name (required), last name, email |
| MPIN | A 4-digit PIN you will use to confirm sensitive actions |
| Shop profile | Your shop's name, its address, and its category |
| Shop details | Keywords, service modes and facilities for your shop |
| KYC | Your verification documents |

Once KYC is verified, your shop becomes **active**.

## What "active" means

Until your shop is active you can keep filling in your profile, but the parts
that involve money are not open to you yet — creating offers, buying promotions,
managing QR codes.

This is not a delay LessPay adds for its own sake. It is what lets money move to
your bank account safely.

## Why the order cannot be skipped

Each step needs the one before it. Your MPIN cannot be set up until the app knows
who you are; your shop cannot be listed until it has a name, an address and a
category; and KYC cannot be verified without the profile it is verifying.

## What if you are stuck on a step

- **The OTP has not arrived.** Wait for the timer to finish and ask for it again.
  Check the number you entered has the right country code.
- **KYC is taking time.** KYC moves from submitted to verified after a review.
  While it says submitted there is nothing more for you to do.
- **You cannot find a step you already filled in.** Everything you entered is
  editable later from your shop profile. Finishing setup does not lock it.
