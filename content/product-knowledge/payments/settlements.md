# When Your Money Reaches Your Bank

A payment and a settlement are two different events. The payment is the customer
paying you. The settlement is that money arriving in your bank account.

## The gap is normal

Money does not move to your bank the instant a customer pays. It is collected
and transferred on a schedule, which your settlements screen shows you — for
example *every day at 6 AM*.

Your settlements summary tells you three things: how much is pending, when the
next transfer goes out, and which bank account it is going to.

## Settlement status

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| Success | The transfer completed. The money is in your account. |
| Processing | The transfer has started and is with the bank. |
| Failed | The transfer did not complete. |

A settlement that is processing has no **UTR** yet — the bank's reference number
only exists once the transfer completes. An empty UTR on a processing settlement
is expected, not a fault.

## Your bank account

Your settlements go to whichever of your bank accounts is marked active. You can
see all your accounts with their numbers partly hidden, and switch which one is
active.

**Switching accounts affects future payouts only.** A settlement already on its
way continues to the account it was sent to. If you change your account today,
today's pending amount may still land in the old one.

## What if a settlement has not arrived

- **Check the status.** Processing means it is with the bank and still moving.
- **Check the schedule.** If your settlements go out daily in the morning,
  money taken this afternoon goes tomorrow, not tonight.
- **Check which account is active.** A recently changed account is the usual
  reason money arrives somewhere unexpected.
- **Failed settlements** normally mean the bank rejected the transfer — usually
  wrong or closed account details.
