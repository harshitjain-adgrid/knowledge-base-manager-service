# Looking at Your Payment History

Your payments list is the record of everything customers have paid you, and it
is what most questions about money come back to.

## The time ranges you can pick

| Range | What it covers |
|---|---|
| Today | Since midnight, Indian time |
| Yesterday | The whole of the previous day |
| This week | The current week so far |
| This month | The current month so far |
| All time | Everything since you started |
| Custom | Two dates you choose yourself |

Every range except custom is worked out for you. You only pick dates when you
choose custom.

## Days are counted in Indian time

The day boundary is midnight in India, wherever you happen to be. A payment
taken at 11:55 pm belongs to that day; one at 12:05 am belongs to the next.

This matters when you are reconciling a day's takings against your own cash
book and a payment seems to be on the wrong side of midnight.

## The summary

Your payments summary shows what came in today and gives you a preview of your
next settlement, so you can see both what you have earned and what is on its way
to your bank without opening two screens.

## Asking Chotu instead

Chotu reads the same numbers. *"How much did I make today?"*, *"aaj kitna aaya"*
or *"what did I take last week"* gets you the answer in a sentence without
opening the list and picking a range.

## What if a total looks wrong

- **Check the source filter.** Showing only LessPay QR payments hides anything
  taken on another provider's QR, and the total looks low.
- **Check the range.** *This week* and *last seven days* are not the same thing.
- **Failed and refunded payments are not earnings.** They appear in the list but
  are not money you keep.
