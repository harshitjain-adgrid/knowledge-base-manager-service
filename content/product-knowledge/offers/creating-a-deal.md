# Creating a Deal

A deal gives the customer extra goods rather than money off their bill — a
second item free, two things bundled at one price, something thrown in. It is
one of the two kinds of offer you can run, and creating one is confirmed with
your MPIN before it goes live.

## The three kinds of deal

Every deal is one of three types, and each asks for different details. Pick the
type first — it decides what the app asks you for next, and it cannot be changed
to a different type afterwards without making a new deal.

## Buy one get one

The customer buys a quantity of something and gets more of it free.

You set how many they buy and how many they get, and whether the free item is
**the same item** they bought or **any item** from your shop. If it can be any
item, you also name the free item.

Example: buy 2 coffees, get 1 coffee free.

## Bundle

Several items sold together at a set price.

You set how many items are in the bundle, the price for the bundle, and which
item it applies to.

Example: any 3 pastries for ₹150.

## Free item

A free item once the bill crosses an amount.

You name the free item and the bill amount that unlocks it. You can also record
what the item costs you, which helps you see whether the deal is worth running.

Example: a free dessert on any bill above ₹500.

## What every deal needs

Whatever the type, a deal needs a **title** and a decision about whether it
**shows on your shop profile**. Quantities are always at least one.

## What if the deal will not save

The app checks the details before it lets you confirm:

- **Buy one get one** needs the item being bought named, and the free item named
  too when it can be any item.
- **A bundle** needs a price.
- **A free item** needs both the item and the bill amount that unlocks it.

If the save button does nothing, one of those is empty.
