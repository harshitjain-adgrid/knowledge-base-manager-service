# Why Your Offer Is Not Showing or Applying

An offer you can see in your list is not always an offer the customer gets.
These are the reasons, in the order worth checking.

## It is not active yet, or not any more

Check the status first. **Scheduled** means the start date has not arrived.
**Expired** means the end date has passed. **Draft** means it was never
finished. Only **active** offers reach customers.

## The customer is not in the audience

If the offer is set to new customers only, a regular will not see it. If it is
set to lapsed customers, someone who came in last week will not see it. This is
the most common cause when an offer works for some people and not others.

## The bill is below the minimum

A discount with a minimum bill does nothing on a smaller bill, and the customer
is not told why. A ₹100-off offer above ₹500 is invisible on a ₹480 bill.

## It is outside the happy hours

A discount with happy hours only applies between the two times you set. If you
also left weekends switched off, it will not apply on Saturday or Sunday even
during those hours.

## Another offer of yours won

Only one offer applies to a payment, and it is the one that saves the customer
the most. A new offer can be working perfectly and still never be seen, because
an older, larger one keeps beating it.

## The limits are used up

If you capped uses per customer, that customer may have used theirs. If you
capped total customers, the offer stops once that many different people have
used it — even though it still shows as active.

## It is hidden from your shop page

*Show on shop profile* controls whether customers browsing your page can see the
offer. With it off, the offer still applies at payment but nobody discovers it
by looking at your shop.

## Still not working

Ask Chotu about the specific offer by name. It can read the offer's settings and
its redemption count and tell you which of these applies.
