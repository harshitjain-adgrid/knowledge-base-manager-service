# What You Pay for a Package

The amount you pay for a package or program is not always a single number. It is
worth understanding what the parts are, because they behave differently.

## The parts of a charge

| Part | What it is | Do you get it back |
|---|---|---|
| Service charge | The fee for the package or service itself | No |
| Deposit | Money held against hardware or equipment | Yes, when returned |

A package normally has only a service charge. A program that includes hardware
can have both.

## GST applies to the service charge only

GST is charged on the service part. **A refundable deposit is not taxed** — it
is your money being held, not a purchase.

So a program with a fee and a deposit is billed as: the fee, plus GST on the
fee, plus the deposit as-is. The GST figure will look smaller than you expect if
you calculate it against the whole total.

## Deposits come back

A deposit is refundable. It is held while you have the equipment and returned
when you give it back in working order. It is not a cost of the service.

## Reading the price before you buy

Every package and program shows the breakdown before you confirm — what the fee
is, what the tax is, and what part is a deposit. Read the breakdown rather than
only the total, especially when a deposit is involved and the headline number
looks larger than the actual cost.

## What if the total looks higher than the advertised price

Two usual reasons: GST has been added to the service charge, or a refundable
deposit is included in the total. The deposit is not money spent.
