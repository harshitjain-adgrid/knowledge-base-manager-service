# What a Promotion Costs

The price of a promotion is worked out from what you choose, and you see the
total before you pay for anything.

## What the price is built from

Every channel has a unit price and a way of counting units:

- **Per day** — you pay for each day it runs.
- **Per 100 customers** — you pay for each block of 100 people reached.
- **Per day per 100 customers** — both together, for placements aimed at a
  particular audience size.

The starting figure is that unit price multiplied by the number of units.

## Buying more gets you a discount

Two discount ladders apply, and they **add together**:

- **Reach** — bigger audiences earn a bigger percentage off.
- **Duration** — longer campaigns earn a bigger percentage off.

Pushes — WhatsApp, text and notification — only get the reach ladder, because
they are sent once and duration does not apply to them.

## GST is added at the end

GST is added to the price after discount. The rate comes from the promotion you
picked, so read the figure shown rather than assuming a rate.

The total you are asked to pay is the discounted price plus GST.

## The limits you can choose within

| Setting | Range |
|---|---|
| Audience | 100 to 10,000 customers |
| Radius around your shop | 1 to 5 km |
| Days | 1 to 30 |

WhatsApp and text promotions always run for a single day, whatever you pick —
they are one-time sends.

## What if the price changes when you confirm

The app shows you a price and the server checks it again when you confirm. If
the two disagree, the campaign is not created and you are asked to look again.

This happens when prices changed between opening the screen and confirming.
Nothing is charged. Reopen the promotion, take the new price, and confirm again.

The check exists so you are never quietly charged something different from what
you were shown.
