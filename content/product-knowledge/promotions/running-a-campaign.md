# Running a Promotion Campaign

Buying a promotion means choosing a channel, setting who and how long, paying,
and letting it run.

## What you choose

1. **The channel** — featured, poster, WhatsApp, text or notification.
2. **The template** — for posters, which screen it appears on.
3. **The offer**, if you want the promotion to point at one.
4. **Who sees it** — the area around your shop and how many people.
5. **How long** it runs.

The cost updates as you change these, so you can see what a wider radius or an
extra day actually costs before committing.

## What happens after you pay

A campaign moves through three states:

| State | What it means |
|---|---|
| Draft | You are still setting it up. Nothing is charged. |
| Awaiting payment | You have confirmed it and payment is in progress. |
| Active | Paid for and running. |

If a payment fails, the campaign goes back to draft rather than disappearing.
Your settings are still there and you can try again.

## Cancelling

Cancelling switches a campaign off. It stays in your list with its history — how
many people it reached, what it cost — rather than vanishing. Nothing in LessPay
deletes your business records outright.

## What if the payment fails

Your campaign returns to draft with everything you entered intact. Try again
from the same campaign rather than building a new one.

If the price has changed since you set it up, you will be asked to accept the
new price before paying.

## What if a campaign is not getting views

- **The radius may be too small.** One kilometre in a quiet area reaches very
  few people.
- **The audience may be set low.** A hundred people is the minimum, and it is a
  small number.
- **The offer behind it may be weak.** A promotion buys attention; the offer is
  what converts it.
