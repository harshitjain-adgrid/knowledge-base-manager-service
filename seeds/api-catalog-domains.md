# Domains

The folder each API lives in. Domains are shaped the way a merchant
thinks about their day, not the way the backend is factored.

- **catalog** (5 APIs) — Products, prices and stock — what the shop sells.
- **offers** (5 APIs) — Discounts, deals and coupon codes.
- **khata** (5 APIs) — Credit ledger — udhaar given, payments received, balances owed.
- **orders** (4 APIs) — Incoming orders and their progress.
- **payments** (4 APIs) — Money in and out — settlements, refunds, payment links.
- **customers** (3 APIs) — The people who buy from the shop.
- **store** (4 APIs) — The shop itself — profile, timings, delivery area.
- **reports** (3 APIs) — Summaries and insights about how the shop is doing.
- **weather** (4 APIs) — Weather, air quality and daylight for a place.
- **reference** (4 APIs) — Look-ups — exchange rates, crypto prices, words, holidays.
